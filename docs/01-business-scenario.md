# 01 - Business Scenario

## Company profile
A mid-size manufacturer of electrical control panels operates one distribution warehouse. The warehouse is managed in SAP S/4HANA EWM, integrated with SAP MM (procurement) and SD (delivery).

## Business problem
- Goods receipts are recorded late, so stock is not visible to production and sales.
- Putaway is manual and depends on operator experience, which causes long search times.
- Picking is not optimized, which leads to walking waste and mis-picks.

## Project goal
Design and document an end-to-end EWM flow that gives real-time stock visibility, directed putaway and guided picking.

## Scope
- Inbound: inbound delivery, goods receipt, putaway
- Internal: stock transfers, physical inventory
- Outbound: outbound delivery order, picking, packing, goods issue

## Out of scope
Transportation management and yard management.

## Success criteria
- Goods receipt posted on the day of arrival
- Putaway completed with system-directed storage bins
- Pick accuracy tracked through warehouse tasks and confirmations
